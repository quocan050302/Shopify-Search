2. TRUST BOUNDARY THREAT MODEL & CRYPTOGRAPHIC LIMITS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Realistic Trust Boundary:
  1) Untrusted DTOs, fixtures, client callers, and test harnesses cannot manufacture LIVE-origin
     evidence through exported APIs.
  2) Direct calls to `persistCollectorReceiptAtomically()` without an internally minted proof token
     or verified attestation automatically demote the origin to `LOCAL_INTEGRITY_ONLY` and set
     `originVerified: false`.
  3) Production proof minting (`mintInternalTrustedInvocationProof()`) is private within the real
     collector module and cannot be invoked externally.
  4) Durable transport attestations survive restart only when signed with authorized production keys.
- Threat-Model Limitation & HMAC Reality:
  A cryptographic HMAC (HMAC-SHA256) establishes integrity only relative to the signer and key;
  it does NOT independently attest to remote network physics or prove third-party server existence.
  A signed attestation proves that the trusted local collector process issued the attestation using
  the configured secret, but cannot protect against a compromised host, arbitrary in-process memory
  mutation, OS kernel compromise, or a stolen signing key.
- Key Rotation & Test Key Gating:
  Test signing keys (e.g. `TEST_PROVENANCE_SIGNING_KEY_32`) and test simulation proofs (`TEST_SIMULATION`)
  fail closed in all production business gates (`strictLiveOrigin: true`), ensuring zero elevation
  of test data to production authoritative provenance.
